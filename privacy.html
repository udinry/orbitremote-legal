Orbit Remote Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

The short version: Orbit Remote is free, has no ads, needs no account, has no analytics, collects no data and sends nothing to any server.

Network use

The app talks only to your TV on your local Wi‑Fi network, using the Android TV Remote protocol over TLS. It does not send anything to the developer or to any other server.

Pairing and the Keychain

Pairing creates a key and certificate that are stored only in this iPhone's Keychain.

Data stored on your device

Saved TV names and IP addresses and your pinned apps stay on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Local Network permission

iOS asks for Local Network access. Orbit Remote uses it only to find and control your TV.

Third parties

There are no third-party SDKs, ads or trackers. Orbit Remote is not affiliated with Google. "Android TV" and "Google TV" are trademarks of Google LLC.
