Orbit Remote

A free remote for Android TV and Google TV, on your iPhone. App Store name: Orbit: Remote for Android TV.

Support

Questions or bugs? Email dev@udbhav.uk and include your iPhone model, iOS version and TV model.

What it works with

Android TV and Google TV devices that have the Android TV Remote Service (most do, including many TVs, streaming boxes and sticks).

How to pair

  1. Put your iPhone and TV on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  2. Open Orbit Remote and pick your TV from the list.
  3. Enter the 6-character code shown on the TV.

Troubleshooting

No TV found / can't connect. Allow Local Network for Orbit Remote in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network (or Settings → Orbit Remote). Make sure the TV and iPhone are on the same network.

Can't turn the TV on from the app. The TV must keep its network connection on in standby. This setting is often called Network standby, Quick start or Wake on Wi‑Fi. Otherwise, use the TV's own remote to turn it on.

The TV stopped responding after a reset. If the TV was reset, pair it again.
